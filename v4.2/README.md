## North star

**One prompt → a working model, app, or site.**
Working product beats dirt-cheap. Avoid multi-agent waste.

- **SOLO** is the default for a clear goal and a small or medium change.
- **PLAN_EXECUTE** is for multi-file work or structure risk.
- Chains of 3 or more agents are not a default.

The decision rule lives in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Detail and evidence: [AGENT-WORKFLOW-POLICY.md](AGENT-WORKFLOW-POLICY.md)
